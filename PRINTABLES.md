1950s License Plates – 19 States, 1625 Plates

Miniature 1950s license plates from the US, Canada and Cuba for 3D Sets Doppler. Two colours, one filament change.

USA: Alaska, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Illinois, Iowa, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Utah and Wisconsin
Canada: Ontario and Alberta
Cuba

<browse-link-banner.jpg>
martincollar.github.io/car-reg-plates

Colours are preset in the 3MF, and the filament change is at 3.8 mm. The 3MFs are set up for a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.1 mm layers and Arachne walls, which works for most plates. A 0.2 mm nozzle gives crisper small text.
